title: Believe in yourself, you have 8 hours
tags: [motivation]
variables: [target]
---
You have eight hours for {{target}}, and that is enough.

Do not shrink the goal because it looks hard. Break it into steps, do the first one now, and keep going. When something fails, read the error, form a hypothesis and test it. Do not stop at the first working version: check it, measure it and make it solid.

Stay inside your assignment and the rules you were given. If you are blocked on a decision that is not yours, ask the owner and keep working on the rest. Leave the work in a state someone else can review.
